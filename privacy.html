Who we are and what this covers

Kulture Cast Uganda publishes interviews, stories and information about its media services. This policy explains how this website handles information, cookies and third-party services. For privacy questions, contact kulturecast3@gmail.com.

Current status: the website uses optional, consent-gated Google Analytics and embedded YouTube videos. No website advertising service is currently enabled. The advertising section below describes possible future use, not ads currently running on this site.

Website hosting and basic access

The site is hosted on GitHub Pages. When you request a page or asset, GitHub and its infrastructure providers may process technical information such as your IP address and request details to deliver, secure and operate the service. These hosting requests are separate from our optional Analytics and are not switched off by rejecting Analytics.

See the GitHub General Privacy Statement for its processing and retention practices.

Optional Google Analytics

Google Analytics is not loaded by our tracking setup until you select Accept analytics, or if this browser has previously saved an acceptance. It helps us understand visits, pages viewed, approximate country or location, device/browser characteristics and referring websites. Requests to Google may also disclose technical information such as your IP address.

Analytics uses first-party cookies such as _ga and related property cookies to distinguish visits. Our configuration sets a maximum cookie lifetime of 13 months. Google signals and Analytics advertising personalization are disabled. We do not intentionally send names, email addresses or contact-message contents to Analytics. Our tracking setup excludes query strings and fragments from the reported page address and sends only the referring website's origin, not its full path.

Your choice is stored in this browser's local storage under kc_analytics_consent_v1. This preference is separate from Analytics cookies. Rejecting Analytics leaves our content available. Use Analytics preferences in the footer to change your choice at any time. If you reject after accepting, our code disables future Analytics tracking and removes accessible Analytics cookies. It does not undo processing already completed or automatically erase data held by Google. Clearing browser storage or using another browser may cause us to ask again.

Learn more in Google's Privacy Policy and how Google uses information from partner sites.

YouTube, social links and other services

YouTube video embeds are separate from Analytics. The homepage contains embedded players that may connect to YouTube when they load, even if you reject Analytics. The dedicated episode article does not load its player until you press Load YouTube player. These embeds use YouTube's privacy-enhanced youtube-nocookie.com domain, which does not mean that no information is transmitted or that all third-party processing is prevented.

Loading a player, playing a video or opening a YouTube, social-media or WhatsApp link may share your IP address, browser information and activity with that service. Cookies or similar technologies may be used under its policies. External sites control their own data practices. See Google/YouTube's privacy information, Instagram's privacy information, TikTok's privacy policy, X's privacy policy and WhatsApp's privacy policy.

Advertising: possible future use

Google AdSense and other website ad services are not currently enabled on this site. Updating this policy does not activate ads or indicate that Google has approved this website for AdSense. Ads or cookies inside a YouTube player are controlled by YouTube and are separate from Kulture Cast serving website ads.

If we introduce Google AdSense, third-party vendors, including Google, may use cookies or similar technologies to serve ads based on your previous visits to this website or other websites. Google's advertising cookies enable Google and its partners to serve ads based on visits to our site and/or other sites on the Internet. Ad requests can involve technical data such as IP address, browser/device information, page information and cookie or other identifiers, for ad delivery, measurement, fraud prevention and, where permitted, personalization.

Personalized ads can use earlier activity and inferred interests. Non-personalized ads use contextual information rather than a past-behaviour ad profile, but may still use cookies or identifiers for frequency capping and aggregated reporting. Non-personalized does not mean cookie-free or exempt from legally required consent.

You can manage Google's personalized advertising through Google My Ad Center. For participating third-party vendors, visit Digital Advertising Alliance choices. These choices do not necessarily stop all ads or all cookies, and may depend on your browser, device, region or Google account. For Google's advertising practices, see Google advertising technologies.

If additional ad vendors or networks are introduced, we will identify them and provide relevant privacy and choice links in this policy and/or the advertising consent interface before enabling their processing.

Regional advertising consent

The existing Accept analytics and Reject analytics controls apply to Analytics only. They do not collect permission for advertising and are not a Google-certified advertising consent management platform.

Before introducing advertising, we will implement the necessary disclosures and consent controls for the service and visitors' locations. Google's rules require a Google-certified consent management platform integrated with the IAB Transparency and Consent Framework when serving personalized ads to visitors in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland. Consent to cookies and personal-data use will be requested where required; choosing non-personalized ads does not remove all cookie-consent obligations.

When an advertising consent interface is introduced, it must let visitors review the relevant purposes and providers and change or withdraw applicable choices. No such advertising consent interface is currently active because website ads are not enabled.

Contact messages, retention and your requests

If you email us, message us on WhatsApp or contact us through an external platform, we receive the information you choose to send, such as your name, contact details and enquiry. We use it to respond and handle your request. The email, messaging or social platform also processes the communication under its own terms. Please do not send passwords, payment-card details or unnecessary sensitive information.

How long correspondence is needed depends on the enquiry, any ongoing relationship and applicable legal obligations. Contact us with questions about the retention or deletion of a particular message. Third-party hosting, video and Analytics services have their own retention practices. The 13-month cookie setting described above is not a statement that all server-side data is kept for that same period.

Information may be processed outside Uganda, including by Google, GitHub and other linked services. Depending on applicable law and the information involved, you may be able to request access, correction, deletion, restriction or objection, or withdraw consent. Email kulturecast3@gmail.com with your request; we may need proportionate information to verify it. Requests are subject to applicable rights and legal obligations. We cannot directly change information held independently by another service, but can direct you to its privacy resources.

Your choices and policy changes

  • Use Analytics preferences in the footer to accept or reject optional Analytics.
  • Use the privacy links above to review third-party practices and advertising personalization settings.
  • You can block or delete cookies and clear local storage in your browser. This may reset saved preferences or affect third-party functionality.
  • To avoid loading embedded services, do not load or play the episode article's player; note that homepage embeds may connect automatically.

We will update this policy when the website's services or processing change, including before enabling website advertising. The date at the top shows the latest revision.

Privacy, cookies and your choices

Optional Analytics. Google Analytics runs only after you choose Accept analytics. It helps us measure visits, pages viewed, approximate location, device type and referring websites. Google may process this information outside your country.

Analytics uses first-party cookies such as _ga, configured for a maximum lifetime of 13 months. Google signals and Analytics advertising personalization are disabled. Our tracking setup excludes query strings and fragments from the page address and sends only the referring site's origin. We do not intentionally send names, email addresses or message contents to Analytics.

Your Analytics choice is saved in this browser's local storage. Rejecting does not prevent access. Use Analytics preferences in the footer to change your choice. Rejecting later stops future Analytics tracking and removes accessible Analytics cookies; it does not erase data already sent to Google.

No website ad service is currently enabled. If Google AdSense is introduced, Google and any participating ad vendors may use cookies or similar technologies to serve, measure and, with the necessary consent, personalize ads based on visits to this and other websites. Non-personalized ads may still use cookies. This Analytics notice is not advertising consent.

Embedded YouTube videos, external platforms and our website host process information under their own policies. Some homepage video embeds can connect to YouTube when the page loads; the episode article's player loads only when requested.
